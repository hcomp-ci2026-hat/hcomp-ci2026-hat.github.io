Attend

Everything you need to know about the venue, registration, accessibility, and getting to Virginia Tech.

Venue

HAT 2026 will be held at Virginia Tech Academic Building One in Alexandria, VA, as part of the co-located HCOMP/CI 2026 conference. The building is located in the Washington, DC metropolitan area.

Building
Virginia Tech Academic Building One
Address
3625 Potomac Ave, Alexandria, VA 22305
Conference
HCOMP/CI 2026 (Sep 27–30)

Registration

HAT 2026 is a workshop at HCOMP/CI 2026. To attend, you must register for the HCOMP/CI conference and select the HAT workshop during registration. Early-bird registration closes August 22, 2026.

Register for HCOMP/CI 2026 →

Travel & Logistics

Getting to Alexandria

By air: The nearest airports are Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA), approximately 15 minutes away, and Washington Dulles International Airport (IAD), approximately 40 minutes away.

By metro: The venue is accessible via the Washington Metro system. Check the HCOMP/CI 2026 website for the nearest station and walking directions.

By car: The venue is accessible via I-395 and the George Washington Memorial Parkway. Visitor parking details will be provided by the conference.
